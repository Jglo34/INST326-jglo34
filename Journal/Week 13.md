This week I used AI to help me reflect on and better understand the idea of navigating ambiguity—a concept that came up in a recent class discussion. I wanted to know what it truly means to work through situations where there is no clear answer, no step-by-step guide, and often conflicting information or expectations. I asked AI how professionals approach ambiguity and what skills are useful for staying grounded and productive in unclear or uncertain environments.
The conversation helped me realize that navigating ambiguity is not just about “figuring it out,” but about embracing curiosity, asking good questions, and being comfortable with not having all the answers. AI introduced strategies like experimenting, iterating quickly, relying on strong core values, and keeping communication open when decisions aren’t black-and-white. This was especially helpful because, as a student and professional, I often encounter unclear instructions or situations where I have to make the best choice without full information.
