Week 12
This week I ended up using AI in a more practical way than usual when one of the charging ports on my MacBook suddenly stopped working. Instead of stressing or going straight to an Apple Store, I asked AI to help me figure out what the issue might be, and it actually walked me through different possibilities like debris in the port, a bent connector, or a logic board issue. It was helpful because it gave me a starting point and made the problem feel less overwhelming, even though I still needed to check things myself. Using AI like that showed me how quickly I’ve gotten used to relying on it for troubleshooting, almost like having a tech-savvy friend who can explain what might be going on before I take the next step.
